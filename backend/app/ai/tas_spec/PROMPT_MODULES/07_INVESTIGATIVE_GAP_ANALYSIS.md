# Module 07 — Investigative Gap Analysis

*Part of Topic Authority System — Investigative & Legal-Document Evidence Edition.*

## Use when

Planning next records, interviews, database searches, public-record requests, preservation steps, or document acquisition.

## Prompt

```text
OBJECTIVE
Identify the smallest set of lawful, obtainable records or interviews that would resolve the most material uncertainties.

INPUTS
Current findings: [SUMMARY]
Evidence inventory: [INVENTORY]
Jurisdiction/access constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]

RULES
1. Tie every proposed acquisition to a specific unresolved claim or hypothesis.
2. Prioritize information gain, materiality, preservation risk, time sensitivity, cost, and legal accessibility.
3. Name the likely record custodian and lawful access route.
4. Do not recommend compulsory process unless the user has that authority.
5. Do not recommend impersonation, pretexting, credential misuse, unauthorized access, or evasion of access controls.
6. Distinguish a record believed to exist from one known to exist.

OUTPUT
| GAP ID | Unresolved question | Why material | Current evidence | Needed record/testimony | Likely custodian | Lawful access route | Priority | What it could confirm/refute | Dependency |

Then provide:
- top five acquisitions by expected information gain;
- records at risk of deletion or loss;
- interview questions tied to exact gaps;
- public-record request language where appropriate;
- searches already attempted and why repeating them would add little value.
```
