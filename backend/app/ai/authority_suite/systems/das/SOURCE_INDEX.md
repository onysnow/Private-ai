## Cross-references to sibling systems (deliberately not re-cited here)

DAS's bias-audit module (04) overlaps with LAS's fallacy work and RAS's bias-disclosure work. Per this system's own evidence-and-precedent discipline (`CORE/DECISION_EVIDENCE_AND_PRECEDENT_POLICY.md` §8), overlapping foundational literature is cross-referenced rather than re-cited independently by every sibling that touches it:

- **RAS** (`systems/ras/SOURCE_INDEX.md`) cites GRADE (RS-01) for its own certainty-of-evidence scale; DAS's evidence-and-precedent policy points to it rather than building a parallel certainty scale for factual questions a decision depends on. RAS's file-drawer-problem citation (RS-03) and replication-crisis citations (RS-04, RS-05) govern whether an underlying research finding a decision relies on is itself trustworthy — a TAS/RAS-layer question DAS does not re-derive.
- **LAS** (`systems/las/SOURCE_INDEX.md`) cites Tversky & Kahneman's conjunction-fallacy work (LS-04) as a reasoning-error distinct from DAS's own anchoring/availability citations (DS-08, DS-09) from the same two researchers' earlier work — LAS's own index notes this split explicitly so neither system re-derives the other's citation. LAS's Toulmin warrant citation (LS-03) is the same "warrant" concept DAS's `EVALUATION/OUTPUT_SCHEMAS.md` criterion-score `*_basis` fields implement without a separate citation.
- Kahneman & Tversky's core heuristics-and-biases literature (DS-08, DS-09) is DAS's own citation for anchoring, availability, and overconfidence as they affect a *decision-maker's* reasoning (Module 04) — distinct from LAS's use of adjacent Tversky/Kahneman work for a *structural reasoning error* (the conjunction fallacy) in an argument's logic. The same researchers, two different failure layers, cited independently by each system for the layer it actually governs.
- **TAS** (`systems/tas/SOURCE_INDEX.md`) defines PE-01 to PE-04 (OpenAI, OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic and Google prompting guidance) and FI-01 (Netherlands Forensic Institute, Local LLM Chat Report Benchmark). The Standard (§2.4, §2.7, §19) and `CORE/DECISION_EVIDENCE_AND_PRECEDENT_POLICY.md` §9 cite them as TAS's; they are not re-indexed here.

## Source-update policy

- Recheck first-party model guidance when the active model, product surface, or model snapshot changes.
- Recheck DS-13 (ICD 203) if the Office of the Director of National Intelligence issues a revision superseding the currently cited version.
- Preserve old citations with retrieval dates; do not silently overwrite them.
- Record a change note when a new source materially changes the operating standard, matching the discipline TAS, RAS, and LAS all apply to their own indices.
