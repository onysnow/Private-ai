## A concept deliberately not given its own numbered citation

**"Steelmanning"** (Modules 01 and 06) is a widely used term in argumentation and rationalist-community writing for constructing the strongest version of an argument before critiquing it, as the deliberate inverse of a straw man. No single, clearly originating academic citation was found for it the way one was for the sources above — it functions more as an established piece of shared vocabulary than a citable published method. LAS uses the term and the practice (which itself has real academic grounding in the general principle of charitable interpretation in argumentation theory) without attributing it to a specific individual source, rather than manufacturing a citation for a term no search has tied to one — the same honesty this doctrine requires of anyone using it.
