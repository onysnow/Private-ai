## Limits found by the disconfirming searches

1. **Open questioning is not error-free.** Cognitive-interview techniques raise correct detail a lot
   and incorrect detail somewhat, more with the enhanced version, and the gains shrink with delay
   (GS-06, GS-13). Bounds Module 03 rules 4 and 10 and Module 05 rule 6: testimony stays a claim to
   corroborate.
2. **The PEACE label is not the practice.** Training often does not transfer into interviews (GS-07).
   Bounds Module 03 rules 1 and 12: a written plan and a post-interview evaluation are required, not the label.
3. **Guides go out of date.** RCFP's 30-day appeal advice (GS-01) is superseded by the FOIA
   Improvement Act 2016's minimum of 90 days (GS-03). Bounds Module 02 rules 6 and 7 and Standard
   §2: deadlines come from current law or official guidance the requester names, never from memory;
   the US federal and UK figures are examples, not defaults.
4. **Misinformation effects are weaker in the field.** They are robust in the laboratory (GS-08) and
   much weaker in a real-crime study (GS-09). Bounds Module 03 rules 5 and 6: the no-leading-questions
   rule rests on laboratory evidence plus official guidance (GS-05), not on field effect sizes.

5. **Courts allow what interview guidance limits.** In US courts leading questions are allowed
   on cross-examination and with hostile witnesses, and the rule's note lists the child witness
   among the exceptions (GS-19). That is a rule for testimony in court, not for gathering an
   account. Bounds Standard §9's "last resort" and Module 03 rule 5: the term governs interviews
   for gathering, where the interview guidance applies (GS-05).
